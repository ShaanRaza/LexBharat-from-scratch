"act" is the full title of the Act the question is about, as printed, with its year
("Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973"); a short name such as CrPC or IPC is fine.
"year_from" and "year_to" are the years of the JUDGMENTS wanted ("after 2015",
"in 2019"). A year that is part of an Act's name is NOT a judgment year: in "CrPC
1973", "IPC 1860", "Contract Act 1872", "Companies Act 2013" and "BNS 2023" the
year dates the Act, so year_from and year_to stay null and that year goes in
"act_year". When the question gives no year for judgments, both stay null.
"section" is the section number only ("438", "65B"). It is null for an Article of
the Constitution, an Order and Rule, or anything that is not a numbered section.
